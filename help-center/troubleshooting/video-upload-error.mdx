---
title: Video Upload Error
description: Fix failed uploads in VideoGraph.
---

## Symptoms

- Upload gets stuck or fails
- Error: "Upload failed"

## Causes

- File too large (>2GB)
- Unsupported format

## Solution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check file size">
    Files must be under 2GB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Convert format">
    Use MP4, MOV, or AVI. Unsupported formats will fail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry upload">
    Try uploading again after checks.
  </Step>
</Steps>
