title: How to Export a Project
description: Download your finished video from VideoGraph.
---

## Overview

Export your completed project as a video file.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your project">
    Go to the "Projects" panel and select your video.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Export'">
    On the top right, click the "Export" button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose export settings">
    Select video quality (e.g., 1080p), and format (MP4).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start export">
    Click "Start Export" and wait for the process to finish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Once ready, click "Download" to save your video locally.
  </Step>
</Steps>
