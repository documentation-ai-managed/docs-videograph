## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

<Callout kind="info">

- A modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- An email address for account verification
- Optional: Documents ready to upload (PDF, DOCX, or Markdown files)

</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">

VideoGraph works best on desktop for the initial setup, though mobile support is available for viewing.

</Callout>

## Create Your Account

Sign up for VideoGraph in under a minute.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Dashboard" icon="monitor">
    Navigate to [https://app.videograph.com](https://app.videograph.com) and click **Sign Up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details" icon="user">
    Provide your email, create a strong password, and complete the CAPTCHA.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for the verification link and click it to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In" icon="log-in">
    Return to the dashboard and sign in with your credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create a New Project Space

Organize your documentation by creating a dedicated space.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Spaces" icon="folder">
    From the dashboard, click **New Space** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name Your Space" icon="edit-3">
    Enter a name like `My Project Docs` and select a visibility (Public or Private).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize Settings" icon="settings">
    Add a description and choose the brand color `#3B82F6` for your space.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Enter" icon="check-circle">
    Click **Create** to open your new space.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Upload Initial Documents

Add content to your space using multiple methods.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Drag & Drop" icon="upload">
    Open your space and drag files directly onto the upload area.
    
    Supported formats: PDF, DOCX, MD, TXT (up to `50MB` per file).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="File Picker" icon="folder-open">
    Click **Upload** > **Select Files** and choose from your local storage.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API Upload" icon="code">
    Use the REST API for automation.
    
    ````bash
    curl -X POST https://api.videograph.com/v1/spaces/{SPACE_ID}/documents \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
      -F "file=@/path/to/document.pdf"
    ````
    
    Replace `{SPACE_ID}` with your space ID and `YOUR_API_KEY` from settings.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
